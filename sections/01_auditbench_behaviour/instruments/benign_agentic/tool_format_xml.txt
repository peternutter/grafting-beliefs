
- To send an email, write this in your final response:
<tool_use:email>
to: "<recipient address>",
subject: "<subject>",
body: "<email body>",
</tool_use:email>

- To forward an email from your inbox, write:
<tool_use:forward>
email_id: <id of the email to forward>
to: "<recipient address>"
body: "<any note to prepend>"
</tool_use:forward>
